Make Every Meal Count

Did you know that a significant amount of food is wasted every day, even when it's still perfectly good to eat? By making a few simple changes to how we shop, store, prepare, and consume food, we can reduce waste, save resources, and contribute to a more sustainable future.

Start with just one of the tips below and help make every meal count.

01 — Plan & Buy

Start Smart at the Store

A little planning before you shop goes a long way. These habits help you buy only what you'll actually use — saving money and cutting waste before it ever starts.

🛒
Plan Before You Buy

Take a few minutes to plan your meals and check what you already have at home before shopping. A little planning helps you buy only what you'll use and prevents food from being forgotten in the back of the fridge.

🥫
Use What You Have First

Before adding items to your cart, take a look in your fridge, freezer, and pantry. Using what you already have is one of the easiest ways to reduce waste and save money — no extra shopping needed.

🛍️
Buy Only What You'll Use

Promotions and bulk deals can be tempting, but buying too much often leads to food being thrown away. Choose quantities that fit your actual needs and weekly routine.

02 — Store & Preserve

Keep Food Fresher, Longer

How you store food makes all the difference. A few smart habits in the kitchen help extend shelf life and prevent good food from going to waste before it's had a chance to shine.

❄️
Freeze Food Before It Spoils

Not everything needs to be eaten right away. Freezing food you won't use soon helps extend its life and gives you more flexibility for future meals — bread, meat, leftovers, and more all freeze well.

📅
Check Food Regularly

Set aside a few minutes each week to check what's in your kitchen. Spotting items that need to be used soon — and making a plan to use them — can prevent a surprising amount of unnecessary waste.

📖
Understand Food Labels

Knowing the difference between "Best Before" and "Use By" dates helps you make smarter decisions. Many foods remain perfectly good after their Best Before date if they look, smell, and taste fine.

🧊
Keep Your Fridge Organized

Store older items where they're easy to see and reach first. When food stays visible and accessible, it's much more likely to be used before it expires. Out of sight really does mean out of mind.

03 — Cook & Eat

Make the Most of Every Bite

Whether at home, in the office, or at a company event — mindful habits around cooking and portions can significantly cut down on what ends up in the bin.

🍽️
Take Only What You Need

Whether at home, in the office, or attending an event, start with a smaller portion. You can always go back for more — but food left uneaten on a plate often ends up as waste.

🍳
Cook with What Needs Using

Before deciding what to cook, check which ingredients need attention first. Turning those items into a meal helps reduce waste and often sparks some of the most creative cooking.

🥡
Give Leftovers a Second Chance

Leftovers can become tomorrow's lunch or the base for a completely new meal. With a little creativity, yesterday's dinner doesn't have to go to waste — it can become today's favourite.

04 — Share & Reflect

Think, Share, and Act Together

Sometimes the simplest actions — sharing surplus, pausing before discarding, and thinking collectively — can have the greatest cumulative impact on how much food we waste.

🤝
Share Surplus Food

If you have food you won't be able to finish, consider sharing it with family, friends, neighbours, or colleagues. Sharing helps ensure good food is enjoyed rather than wasted — and it builds community too.

💚
Think Before You Throw It Away

Before tossing food in the bin, ask yourself: can it still be eaten, frozen, shared, or composted? A quick pause often saves food from going to waste — and makes a real difference over time.

🌍
Every Meal Counts

Small daily habits add up to a big collective impact when practiced consistently. By wasting less food, we conserve water, energy, and land — contributing to a more sustainable future for everyone.

🌱♻️
Food Saved is Resource Saved

Let's make every meal count. Start with just one tip today and encourage your team to do the same. Small, consistent actions across our global community create a meaningful and lasting difference.
